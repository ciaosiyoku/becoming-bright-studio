## Next, in this order

1. **The child test** (step 7). Change the teach card or the feedback for what it shows, then test again.
2. **Native-speaker readings** of German, French and Yoruba, sentence by sentence (app text and skill texts).
3. **Real devices:** an Android or Fire tablet and an iPad: install, read-aloud voices, print, file backup.
4. Check the new skills' placement (levels) against the school frameworks, and test the harder ones (Stages 4–7)
   with older children.
5. More skills only after these work with children (e.g. number bonds to 10, halves, CVC words).

6. **Check the facts** in 100 Whys, Did you know? and places against a source, and confirm the radio stations'
   terms for third-party players.
